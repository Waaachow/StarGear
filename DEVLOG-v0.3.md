# StarGear — Episode 0: The Ghost Signal — v0.3

This update is all about making the sector feel *alive* — a living enemy fleet you can read, hunt, and avoid — plus a new codex for studying the ships that are trying to kill you.

## 🛰️ New: Ship Database
A new **Ship Database** in the ship menu. Every hostile hull — and the Command Nexus itself — starts as a locked silhouette. Scan an enemy in battle (Kael's **Scan**) and its entry fills in: sprite, class, full stats, and a tactics briefing on how it fights and how to beat it. Complete the codex as you survive encounters.

*(A fresh game starts with an empty database — your discoveries carry through a playthrough.)*

## 🔴 New: A Living Sector
Hostiles no longer blink in and out right on top of you. The sector now holds a small **standing patrol** of enemy ships that roam on their own:

- **They persist.** Fly past one and it's still out there — turn around and give chase.
- **They show on the Star Map.** Any hostile in charted space appears as a pulsing red contact, so you can pick your fights or plot a course around trouble.
- **They're avoidable.** A ship only engages once you get close — seeing it coming is half the battle.

## 🚀 New: Every Hull Handles Differently
Each of the six enemy hulls now has its own **cruise speed**. The lumbering **Tank** crawls across the sector while the **Wing** and **Neo** interceptors dart — you can gauge a contact's threat partly by how fast it's moving. Every ship's speed is listed in its Ship Database entry.

And when a fight breaks out, **the ship you ran into is guaranteed to be in the battle** — no more ramming a Tank and squaring off against a squad of something else.

## 👥 New: Crew Dossiers
Open any crew member's sheet to read their **full biography** beside their portrait, and see them by their **full name** — Captain Elias Voss, Dr. Kael Mercer, Chief Engineer Tessa Rourke, and the rest of the StarGear's crew.

## 🧭 Quality of Life
- Reaching the edge of the sector now **spins you around and nudges you one step back inward**, instead of dead-stopping against the rim.

Thanks for flying with the StarGear — more to come. 🌌
