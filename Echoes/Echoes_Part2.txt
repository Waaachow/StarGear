Gameplay Between Part 2 & 3

Instead of immediately revealing Astra's past, the player returns to space.

They now have access to:

Optional scans.
Mercenary contracts.
Merchant escort.
Pirate cleanup missions.

After completing one or two optional activities, Rowan calls over comms.

Rowan (Radio)

"Captain."

"We've got company."

Tessa

"Multiple hyperspace signatures."

Kael

"...A fleet."

Rowan

"Get back here."

Gameplay

Fly back to Cogwheel Station.

As the player approaches...

Large ships begin dropping out of hyperspace one by one.

No dialogue.

Just the visual.

The player already knows—

Something very bad has arrived.

I think this is much stronger than having Raze immediately appear after the relay. It gives the player a brief period to absorb the mystery, complete a couple of optional activities, and enjoy the sector before the mood shifts dramatically. When they finally return to Cogwheel Station and watch an enemy fleet emerge from hyperspace in real time during exploration, the transition into the next story chapter feels much more cinematic because the player witnesses the invasion rather than being told it has begun.
