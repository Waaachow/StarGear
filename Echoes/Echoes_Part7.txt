END OF EPISODE 1

I actually prefer this ending over the previous version because it gives Voss one final defining moment. His decision to rescue A-17 isn't tactical—it's moral. His line, "If we leave her... we become him," encapsulates the entire theme of the episode. Raze sees androids as property; Voss sees people. That choice is what transforms A-17's future and leaves the audience with a clear understanding of why the StarGear crew is different. It also sets up a stronger season arc by revealing that the true enemy's interest extends beyond Astra to Voss himself, hinting that his past rescue of Astra may have far-reaching consequences.
