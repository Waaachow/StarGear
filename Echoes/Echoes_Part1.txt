Why this works better

Notice how almost nothing is narrated. The player learns everything organically:

Helios Reach is peaceful because the crew reacts to bustling traffic.
Crimson is a returning friend because everyone already knows how to respond to him.
Rowan is competent because he lays out evidence instead of a narrator explaining the situation.
The investigation is motivated by a tangible briefing and holographic map, not exposition.

The result feels more like an anime episode: characters carry the story, while the visuals and gameplay communicate the world around them.
