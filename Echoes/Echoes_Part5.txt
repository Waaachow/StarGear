Gameplay
Exploration

A new search area opens around the Nebula.

Objectives:

Scan hyperspace residue.
Investigate false signatures.
Locate the hidden Android Factory.

The player is given freedom to explore, with the story advancing only after they've pieced together the trail. This makes the discovery of the factory feel earned rather than simply marked on the map.
