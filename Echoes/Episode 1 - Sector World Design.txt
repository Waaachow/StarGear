Episode 1 - Sector World Design
Overview

Episode 1 takes place entirely within a single handcrafted sector of space.

The sector should feel like an active frontier region with industry, commerce and infrastructure. Every location should have a believable purpose within the sector, creating the impression that it exists independently of the player.

The environment should favour quality over scale. Rather than a vast procedural map, this sector should consist of a small number of carefully designed locations connected by open space.

The locations should feel naturally connected, with the surrounding space acting as the travel corridors between them.

Trade Lane

The central artery of the sector.

This area represents the safest and busiest part of local space.

Visual Identity
Constant civilian traffic
Cargo freighters
Ore haulers
Passenger transports
Navigation buoys
Automated maintenance drones
Security patrol craft

The area should feel alive, with ships following predictable routes and passing one another naturally.

Main Station

The largest structure in the sector.

This serves as the commercial and industrial centre of the region.

Visual Identity
Large rotating habitation ring
Multiple docking bays
Cargo cranes
Repair docks
Service craft moving around the station
Bright navigation lighting
Heavy industrial architecture

The station should immediately stand out as the centrepiece of the sector.

Asteroid Mining Belt

A large asteroid field under active industrial operation.

Visual Identity
Dense asteroid clusters
Mining lasers
Automated drills
Cargo containers
Ore processing equipment
Large mining barges
Excavation platforms

The area should feel busy but hazardous, with industrial equipment built directly into the asteroids.

Communications Relay

A small orbital infrastructure platform positioned away from the main traffic routes.

Visual Identity
Large communications arrays
Solar panels
Long-range antennae
Docking arm
Maintenance walkways
Exterior lighting
Utility modules

Its isolated position should contrast with the activity around the station.

Mercenary Hangar

A privately operated outpost built into a hollowed asteroid.

Visual Identity
Reinforced docking platform
Workshop structures
External repair gantries
Storage containers
Fuel tanks
Exposed maintenance equipment
Makeshift construction

It should feel practical rather than military, with a rough, independent character.

Nebula

A dense cloud formation occupying the edge of the sector.

Visual Identity
Bright coloured gas clouds
Thick dust formations
Electrical discharges
Reduced visibility
Floating debris
Distorted lighting

The nebula should create a dramatic change in atmosphere compared to the open space elsewhere in the sector.

Hidden Installation

A massive industrial complex concealed deep within the nebula.

Visual Identity
Gigantic rotating construction rings
Heavy armour plating
Large docking ports
Massive internal hangars
Defensive satellite network
Dense mechanical detail
Red warning lights throughout

The installation should be one of the largest structures seen so far, giving the impression of enormous manufacturing capability.

Jump Gate

A permanent hyperspace gateway located on the outer edge of the sector.

Visual Identity
Large circular gate structure
Navigation beacons
Long-range communication towers
Minimal surrounding traffic
Clean, symmetrical architecture

Its isolated location should reinforce that it is the primary route into and out of the sector.

Open Space

The empty space between major locations is just as important as the locations themselves.

It should never feel completely empty.

Include subtle environmental details such as:

Distant planets and moons
Starfields
Small asteroid clusters
Derelict satellites
Wreckage drifting through space
Navigation buoys
Cargo containers
Civilian traffic
Passing patrol ships
Light nebula haze in distant regions

These details should create the impression that the sector is inhabited and continuously operating.

Visual Progression

The sector should evolve over time without changing its fundamental layout.

Examples include:

New debris fields appearing
Damage to existing structures
Increased repair activity
Additional emergency lighting
More visible patrols
Burned-out ships drifting through trade lanes
Repair vessels working around damaged infrastructure

These changes should make the world feel responsive and lived-in.

Art Direction

The overall aesthetic should evoke a vibrant, anime-inspired space opera rather than a hard science-fiction simulation.

The sector should feel colourful, optimistic and adventurous, with exaggerated silhouettes, bold lighting and memorable landmarks. Every major location should be instantly recognisable from a distance through its shape, scale and colour palette, making navigation intuitive without relying heavily on interface elements.
